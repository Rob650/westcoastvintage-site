About

Curated by Julian

West Coast Vintage is an independent vintage shop founded and curated by Julian — Bay Area born and raised, now based in Portland, Oregon.

He hand curates up and down the coast — Portland, San Francisco, Los Angeles — sourcing vintage sportswear, graphic tees, jackets, hats, workwear and unique pieces that reflect his personal eye for nostalgic streetwear and West Coast style.

Every piece is selected individually — never treated like bulk inventory.

Buy, sell and trade available. Nationwide shipping available.
